GLB to STL converter

Convert a .glb or .gltf model into a printable STL or a plain OBJ. The file is parsed with the three.js glTF loader (Draco and meshopt compression included), turned Z-up, scaled from meters to millimeters and set on the build plate, all inside your browser. No GLB yet? FurniMesh's image to 3D generator, made by the same team, builds one from a photo of a furniture piece.

Drop a .glb or .gltf file here

For a .gltf with a separate .bin and textures, select all of its files together. Draco and meshopt compression are supported.
